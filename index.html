Quiz and assembly

Now try it with your own hands

Reading about a board is one thing; finding the parts yourself is what makes it stick. Both exercises run on the 3D model at the top of the page and work for every board.

Quiz · about a minute

Find the part

We name a part, and you find it on the model and click it. Rotate the board, zoom in and flip it over, just like the real thing.

  1. Read the question. “Find the microcontroller, the brain of the board.”
  2. Click the part. Not sure? Take a hint, or skip and see the answer.
  3. See your score. Only first-try answers without a hint count.

Assembly · a few minutes

Take it apart, put it back together

The board comes apart into a tray of parts on the bench. Put every chip, connector and header back where it belongs and solder it down.

  1. Pick a part from the tray. Chips, connectors, headers, buttons.
  2. Place it on its footprint. The pads on the bare board show where it goes.
  3. Solder it in. When the last joint is done, the board is whole again.
